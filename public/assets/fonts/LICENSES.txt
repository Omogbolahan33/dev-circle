Survey type library — licences
==============================

These are Latin subsets of open-source families, redistributed here rather
than loaded from a font CDN so that a member answering a survey never has to
make a request to a third party to read a question.

Each licence permits redistribution, including bundled with an application,
provided the licence travels with the font. That is what this file is.

The subsets were produced by Google Fonts and fetched from their CDN; the
files themselves are unmodified.
