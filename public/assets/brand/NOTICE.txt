Credit Direct brand assets
==========================

creditdirect.svg        The "credit direct" lockup, Denim blue #107EBC.
creditdirect-white.svg  The same artwork with the ink swapped to #FFFFFF,
                        for dark surfaces. Blue reads at 3.84:1 on the dark
                        sidebar (#1A1A2E) and 4.44:1 on the light one; white
                        reads at 17.06:1 on the dark. Hence two files.
favicon.svg             The mark on a white rounded badge, for the browser tab.

Source
------
Retrieved 2026-08-31 from https://www.creditdirect.ng/, which serves them from
its own CDN. They are Credit Direct Limited's trademarks, used here in Credit
Direct's own software. They are not covered by this repository's licence and
should not be reused elsewhere.

Editing
-------
creditdirect-white.svg is generated from creditdirect.svg by replacing #107EBC
with #FFFFFF. If the lockup is ever replaced, regenerate the white variant the
same way rather than downloading a second file, so the two cannot drift apart.
